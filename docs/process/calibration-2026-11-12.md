# First calibration of the team descriptions, 12 Nov 2026

Four exam rounds on the 29-item golden set, with the live prompts. P/R is precision / recall: of what we send, how much the team should get; and of what the team should get, how much we send.

| Round | Change | Product P/R | Marketing P/R | R&D P/R | Severe misses |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Starting descriptions | 59 / 100 | 78 / 95 | 93 / 100 | 0 |
| 2 | Strict Product wording ("restated capabilities are not for them") | 100 / 69 | 82 / 95 | 93 / 100 | 1 (Marketing) |
| 3 | Middle-ground Product wording, Marketing urgency spelled out | 83 / 77 | 82 / 95 | 87 / 100 | 1 (Marketing) |
| 4 | Quote retry and same-story link grading (decision 0009), press-release key fixed | 91 / 77 | 79 / 100 | 100 / 100 | 0 |

Round 4 is the baseline.

## What each round taught

- **Round 1.** Product's phrase "signals of where their roadmap is heading" matched almost anything, so Product was sent too much. Marketing's list "(launches, acquisitions, awards, research headlines)" made awards and research urgent.
- **Round 2.** Too strict. Product stopped getting real changes. This is the usual overcorrection: precision went up, recall went down.
- **Round 3.** Balanced. The one remaining severe miss was a correct alert dropped for an inexact quote: a builder problem, not a description problem.
- **Round 4.** That problem is fixed. One answer key was also wrong: the press release did add news for R&D. The judge was right, and the key was changed.

## What is left

- **Product** misses three grey cases (funding with a regional expansion, supply-chain framing, an inventory framework). The judge reads them as framing, not capability.
- **Marketing** sometimes gives research and framing a digest where the key says log.
- Both are owner questions. The Slack loop (decision 0011) is meant to ask them.
