# Demo script (five minutes)

The video and every day's plan follow this script. A step is done only when it runs on the live URL with real data, or with the canary where noted.

| # | Time | What the viewer sees | Proves | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 0:00–0:30 | The problem: four competitors, three teams, too much noise | Framing | Not started |
| 2 | 0:30–1:30 | One real competitor post in Slack: Product and R&D each get it with a different reason and quote; Marketing doesn't, and the feed shows why | Per-team judgment with evidence | Not started |
| 3 | 1:30–2:15 | A quiet page change (canary, changed on purpose) arrives as a before/after with an explanation; a cosmetic change is shown as not sent | Changed pages, noise control | Not started |
| 4 | 2:15–3:15 | The owner adds HiddenLayer by typing its domain, sees the sources and recent items found, adds the topic "MCP security" for R&D, sets Marketing to quiet | Self-service without engineering | Not started |
| 5 | 3:15–4:00 | A "not useful" vote with a reason; the operator view shows per-team precision on the golden set and the usefulness rate | Output quality and how it improves | Not started |
| 6 | 4:00–4:40 | The operator view flags a broken source and a missed run, with cost per run | Runs unattended, 3 a.m. failures | Not started |
| 7 | 4:40–5:00 | What was left out and what comes next | Judgment about scope | Not started |

## Golden examples the demo depends on

To be filled from real items during the fetch spike: at least one post that matters to several teams for different reasons, one that matters to one team only, one quiet page change, one cosmetic change, and one weak claim that should be withheld.
