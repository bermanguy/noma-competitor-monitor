This is a pricing and packaging page. The changes were computed by comparing the plans, prices and features we read last time with what the page shows now.
What usually matters:
- A new or removed plan, or a renamed one: a change in who they sell to.
- A price change, a new free or entry tier, or a move between per-seat, per-agent and usage pricing.
- A feature or limit moving between plans (for example SSO, number of agents, or runtime protection moving down to a cheaper plan): a change in what they consider premium.
- A switch from published prices to "contact sales", or the reverse.
A change in wording that keeps the same plans, prices and features is not news.
